Mission-Critical UX

Designing for high-stakes cyber operations.

At Parsons, supporting U.S. Cyber Command, I owned UX end to end across multiple mission-critical programs: research, wireframes, design systems, usability testing, interface design and front-end work for the analysts, operators and administrators who rely on them.

My role

End-to-end UX on every program

The same process carried across each program, whether or not it had a user interface.

  • Research

    Learning how analysts, operators and administrators work, and where their tools get in the way.

  • Wireframes

    Mapping workflows and screens before committing to visual design.

  • Design systems

    Shared components and patterns so programs look and behave consistently.

  • Usability testing

    Putting designs in front of real users and refining them from what we learned.

  • Interface design

    High-fidelity interfaces for the programs with a user interface.

  • Front-end

    Helping turn the designs into working, production interfaces.
